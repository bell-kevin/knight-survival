How to play

Every yellow coin is a point for the whole team. Every 5 points another red knight shows up, and shooting one is worth 10. Collect enough coins and the door opens: walk through it and everybody goes on to the next level. Touch a red knight and you're out: press any key or tap to jump back in. If everybody is out, the world starts over at level 1.

How it works

There is no game server and no account. The game is C++ compiled to WebAssembly. Your browser finds the other players in your room through public Nostr relays, then talks to them directly with WebRTC. One player's browser runs the world; if they leave, another player takes over and the score carries on.

Privacy and freedom

No cookies, no analytics, nothing stored. Like any peer-to-peer game, the players in your room (and the relays that introduce you) can see your IP address. The whole game is free software under the GNU GPL v3: read it, change it, share it.
