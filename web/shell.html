How to play

Every gold coin is a point for the whole team, and every 5 coins another bad guy shows up. Collect enough coins and the door opens: walk through it and everybody goes on to the next of the 8 levels. Every touch from a bad guy takes some of your health; when it runs out you're out, so press any key or tap to jump back in. If everybody is out, the world starts over at level 1.

The bad guys

Red knights go down with one shot (10 points). Green scouts are small and quick but weak (15). Purple champions take 3 shots and hit harder (30). Bronze brutes are big and slow, take 8 shots and hit very hard (60). And in the last hall waits the warlord: 25 shots, and one touch knocks you out (250).

How it works

There is no game server and no account. The game is C++ compiled to WebAssembly. Your browser finds the other players in your room through public Nostr relays, then talks to them directly with WebRTC. One player's browser runs the world; if they leave, another player takes over and the score carries on.

Privacy and freedom

No cookies, no analytics, nothing stored. Like any peer-to-peer game, the players in your room (and the relays that introduce you) can see your IP address. The whole game is free software under the GNU GPL v3: read it, change it, share it.
